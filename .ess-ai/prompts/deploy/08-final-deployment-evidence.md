# Final Deployment Evidence

_When to use:_ Use to close the release record.

```text
Prepare the final deployment evidence report.
Include:
1. Version/commit deployed
2. Environment
3. Deployment time/result
4. Approval evidence
5. CI/test results
6. Smoke-test results
7. Database/configuration changes
8. Monitoring/log observations
9. Issues encountered
10. Rollback status
11. Remaining risks
12. UNKNOWNs
Separate actual evidence from assumptions. Do not mark deployment successful without evidence.
```
