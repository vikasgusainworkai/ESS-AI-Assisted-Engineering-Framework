# Review Ai Change Risk

_When to use:_ Before approving a significant AI-generated change.

```text
Review this change from a lead/manager perspective. Identify architecture risk, security risk, customer impact, database/API impact, regression risk, change-size risk, maintainability concerns and testing gaps. Classify each as Low/Medium/High based on evidence and explain why. Do not approve or reject the change; provide the facts needed for review.
```
